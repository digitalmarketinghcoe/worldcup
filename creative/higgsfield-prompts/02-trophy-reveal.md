# Higgsfield Prompt — Trophy Reveal Video

**Usage:** Plays in the prediction success state / countdown section. 4K 60fps HDR, 8–10s.

## Prompt

Extreme slow-motion macro reveal of a golden World Cup style trophy emerging from total darkness. A single overhead spotlight ignites, sweeping down the trophy's polished gold surface, liquid light reflections rolling across the metal. Particles of golden dust orbit the trophy in zero-gravity drift. Background pure void black fading to deep navy (#020617) with faint crimson rim light (#D90429) on one edge. Camera orbits 30 degrees around the trophy, rising slowly. Condensation glint, fingerprint-free pristine gold, jeweler-grade macro detail. Broadcast-quality, HDR speculars blooming softly.

**Camera:** 100mm macro orbit, slow crane up, f/2.8.
**Lighting:** Single key spotlight from above, crimson rim, gold bounce.
**Mood:** Sacred. The prize everything is fought for.

## Negative prompt

hands, people, text, scratches, plastic look, flat reflections, fast motion
