# Higgsfield Prompt — Hero Background Video

**Usage:** Looping background behind the landing hero. Export 4K 60fps HDR, 12–16s seamless loop, no audio needed (ambience handled in-app).

## Prompt

Ultra-realistic cinematic shot inside a colossal night-time football stadium, camera slowly dollying forward at pitch level through volumetric haze. Stadium floodlights cut god-rays through drifting mist. 90,000 fans as soft bokeh light points in deep navy darkness (#071124). Confetti micro-particles catch golden light (#FFD60A). Slow motion 120fps feel, shallow depth of field, anamorphic lens flares from floodlights, broadcast-quality sports production. Color grade: deep midnight blues with crimson (#D90429) accent glow from LED perimeter boards. No players visible, no text, no logos. Seamless loop.

**Camera:** Slow dolly-in, 24mm anamorphic, f/1.8, pitch-level.
**Lighting:** Volumetric floodlights, HDR highlights, fog density 30%.
**Mood:** Anticipation before kickoff. Electric, reverent, massive.

## Negative prompt

text, watermarks, logos, players' faces, daytime, flat lighting, cartoon, oversaturation
