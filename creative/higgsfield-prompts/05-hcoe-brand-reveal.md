# Higgsfield Prompt — HCOE Brand Reveal

**Usage:** Closing brand sting / social video outro. 4K 60fps HDR, 6–8s. Composite the actual HCOE white logo (public/Himalaya_Logo_White.png) in post at the final hold.

## Prompt

Cinematic macro shot of glowing circuit-board traces in deep navy (#071124) pulsing with golden energy (#FFD60A), the traces flow and converge like stadium aisles seen from above, morphing into the chalk lines of a football pitch viewed top-down. The center circle ignites in a soft golden flare. Particles rise from the pitch like stadium fireflies, swirling into a clean negative space at frame center, ready for a logo composite, held for 3 seconds. Volumetric glow, premium tech-keynote aesthetic, HDR bloom, slow confident motion. Color palette: midnight navy, gold, single crimson (#D90429) energy pulse.

**Camera:** Top-down macro to slow pull-back crane, 50mm.
**Lighting:** Self-illuminated traces, soft gold bloom, vignette edges.
**Mood:** "Engineering meets football." Precision becoming passion.
